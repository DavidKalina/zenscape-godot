# Pastoral downs (two takes)

**Intent:** gentle rolling chalk-down hills, drifts of wildflowers and
dandelions over the slopes, an oak or two, and a little pond in a dip. Wind
blown across with the Feather. Questions: does the gentle end look different
from a fresh plate, and how does the Feather read in stills?

**Recipe:** `tools/landscapes/pastoral_downs.gd` (take 2). Paddle at size 3:
four swells to 2.4, 1.6, 1.2 and 1.8 m, and three pats on the saddles. From the
overview, a size-2 swell down to sink the pond spot to about 0.3 m, then a
1.5 s water ball. Four wildflower and dandelion drag-scatters, two wildflower
taps by the pond, and one short oak flick. 12 s of world at 4x. Feather at
size 3: two long drags across the plate, with a still taken mid-drag.
- Take 1: the same hills, but the pond hollow failed (see below), two oak
  flicks were sown, and the world ran for 25 s.

**Stills:** /private/tmp/zs-landscapes/pastoral_downs/ (01-fresh, 02-downs,
03-downs-side, 04-meadow, 05-flowers-close, 06-wind-mid-drag, 10-downs-low,
11-pond). Take 1 is in `pastoral_downs-take1/`.

## What came out
- **The hills:** hold_to hit every target within 0.1 m. Their shape is just
  right: round shoulders, soft feet, no horn. But at 1.2-2.5 m on a 27 m plate,
  **they barely read from the building view**. 02-downs looks like a fresh
  plate with a slight swell. The low side view (03-downs-side) shows the
  roll clearly. The faceted green shading is the same on flat and slope, so
  nothing marks a crest from a hollow except the shadow at low sun.
- **The pond:** take 1's hollow did not take. The hold was pressed from a
  close view after the pats, and 402 px of pull did nothing. The ball landed
  on a 1.2 m crest and soaked away (0 m³). In take 2, pressed from the
  overview, it worked: 3.0 m³ over 16 m², 0.36 m deep.
- **The flowers:** about 100-130 flower heads (daisy, spike, umbel,
  button). They read only in the close still (05-flowers-close), as small
  stalks with butterflies. From any building-distance view they are
  invisible: no colour drift, no patch of yellow.
- **Trees take over.** Take 1, with two oak flicks and 25 s: about 110 trees.
  The downs became a mixed forest (04-meadow, 07-downs-low). Take 2, with one
  short flick and 12 s: still about 70 trees, broadleaf, spruce and birch,
  spread over every hill. The oak sowing did lean broadleaf, and oaks appear
  on the west hill. But the **open grassland** that defines downs never
  survives, so the landscape converges on "forest on lumps".
- **The Feather:** both drags sent wakes (`feather_sent` {wake: 2}). In the
  mid-drag still (06), the wind shows as white streak lines and a soft puff
  of cloud under the feather, which reads clearly as wind. In any still taken
  after release it is gone: the trees and grass show no lasting lean, no
  flattened grass trail and no blown petals. **In stills the Feather is
  invisible.** Its effect is motion, so judge it in a movie.

## How the tools felt
- **Paddle swell:** the tool for this landscape, and it's good. hold_to
  landed every height in 40-90 px of pull. The 6.5 m radius (9.75 m at size
  3) fits the scale of downs.
- **Paddle pat:** softens saddles subtly, as you'd hope.
- **Flower scatter:** easy to lay. But a drag gives at most 8 drops, and
  drops are a few stalks, so a "drift" across a hillside takes many strokes.
- **The gentle end:** compared with a fresh plate, the gentle end is gently
  different in form and not at all in colour or texture.

## Gaps
- **No ground colour from what's sown.** A meadow drift that tints the ground
  (yellow, white, purple), as the clay tints dirt and rock, would make
  flowers readable at plate scale.
- **No way to hold open ground.** Grazing, or a "meadow" material that
  outcompetes seedlings, is missing. The world's emergence plants trees on
  every open slope.
- **No lasting trace of wind.** A wind-flattened grass trail, or trees that
  lean after a strong gust, would give stills something.
- **Bug-ish:** a hold pressed after the camera moved close did not register
  (twice across my builds, here and in zen_garden take 1).

## Scores
Buildable 4/5 (hills easy) · Looks 3/5 (pretty forest; not downs) · Matched intent 2/5
