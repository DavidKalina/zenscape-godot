# Loose-object mischief

Deliver all three object families through cursor grabbing and flinging:

- Stones: quick tosses, light bonks/flinches/glances, and pond skipping.
- Logs: tumbling and rolling, foot-sweeping impacts and recovery, and buoyancy.
- Selected boulders: heavy dragging, downhill rolling, and Bigfoot noticing
  danger and scrambling away.
- Objects dropped nearby invite Bigfoot to investigate after settling.

Keep the rest of the preset composed and anchored. Preserve tree, wind,
water-dunking, autonomous life, camera, lighting, pause, reset, cancellation,
and tool switching. Maintain the goofy, resilient tone without injuries.

Completion requires meaningful physics and interaction tests, regression
checks, visual inspection of each behavior, documented controls/limitations,
and demonstration footage verified for capture hitching. The full milestone is implemented and verified. See `validation.json`,
`video-pacing.json`, and the local `loose-mischief.mp4` demonstration.
