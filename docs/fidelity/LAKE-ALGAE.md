# Lake algae

The native lake now uses the original `lake-algae.js` color block, generated by
`tools/export_lake_algae.py`. The live material exporter records amount, richness,
seed and all twelve debris anchors; the geometry importer preserves those values.
Seed 7 has amount 1, richness 0.2254100953, seed 2.2002695396, and no active anchors.

The film drifts along wind, forms elongated noise patches and lee accumulations,
thins in strong wind, fades under ice, and excludes dry shores and cut walls.
It uses the paused water clock and appears after lake growth passes 0.97.
`--no-algae` disables the film for comparisons.

## Validation

Native Forward+ GPU `tests/test_lake_algae.gd` passes imported parameter/anchor
checks, visible film over a broad spatial field, exact held-clock stability,
drift, strong-wind thinning, and freeze/growth/shore/cut rejection. The complete
scene rendered 30 frames at the close lake camera without shader errors; its
last frame was inspected. The broad-field test establishes behavior, not matched
source pixels or the visibility of film at every saved lake coordinate.

Source captures `lake-material-source.png` and
`lake-material-source-no-algae.png` show that disabling algae does not remove
the source lake's overall green tone. The native lake remains bluer with softer
shadows. Algae therefore does not resolve that mismatch. Source reflection mixes
after colorspace/tone mapping; native reflection currently mixes in HDR, which
remains a separate fidelity gap. Underwater sight override is disabled in this
source scene and has not been ported here.

Follow-up: `LAKE-SHADOWS.md` identifies and corrects the missing source lake
shadow-receiving flag, substantially improving the shadowed-water color reported
in this earlier pass. Reflection and compositing order remain separate gaps.
