Does lighting give the scene more depth?

Actual Godot Forward+ renders, 1920 × 1200. Geometry, colors, camera, time and atmosphere are held fixed within each view. Production defaults are unchanged.

The tested changes are subtle. Reducing fill mostly darkens existing shadows. Routing hemisphere light through ambient irradiance makes contact shading work, but SSAO plus screen-space indirect light does not substantially change the overall scene.
