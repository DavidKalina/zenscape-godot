# Bigfoot inhabitant goal

The playable loop is implemented: notice, approach at a fitting pace, inspect,
rest and wander. Carrying and wind interrupt safely; editing reconsiders routes.

## Completed validation

- Grounded Blender walking, continuous gait across path corners, curious 0.5 m/s,
  wandering 0.85 m/s and excited 1.5 m/s approaches.
- Question/exclamation reactions, inspection pauses and interest cooldowns.
- Bounded navigation avoiding water, steep slopes, props and chunk edges.
- Live edits, undo/redo, no-ground waiting, held-safe fallback and recovery.
- Repeated carry/gust interruptions, pause/reset and autonomous resumption.
- Rendered 16-second reel: curiosity, excitement, obstacle ahead and reaction.
- Computer-use inspection of pause, Build mode and a live terrain edit.
- Continuous brush regression reproduced and unnecessary water/scenery work removed.

Final renderer regression results and before/after measurements are recorded in
README.md and the accompanying JSON reports. Future climbing, social behavior,
object-use animations and a dedicated run clip remain outside this first loop.
