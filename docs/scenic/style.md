# Style rule: faceted solids, soft everything that flows or glows

Settled 2026-10-01 on `beautification`, from the two concept-art sheets in the repo root.

The game mixes a faceted, low-poly look with a painterly one. They do not clash when the split follows what a thing is:

- **Faceted:** solid things with edges. Mountains, rocks, cliffs, floating debris, trees, people, boats and props. Their silhouettes are cut in clear planes.
- **Soft:** things that flow, move or shine. Water, fog, mist, sky, cloud, fire, lantern glow, wind and light itself. These are smooth and painterly.
- **In between:** grass and flowers. Their shapes are faceted: flat, clearly cut petals and simple stems, readable at diorama distance. Their light and motion are soft: petals glow when backlit, grass pales as a gust rolls over it, and heads nod and sway smoothly.

Water stays glassy and smooth. It fits the faceted world by reflecting and tinting from it, not by being faceted itself.

## What ties the two sides together

- One sun, one fog, one colour grade and the shared palette families for everything.
- Consistent rules within a category: no tree shaded smooth beside another shaded faceted, no brush-stroke texture on one rock and clean facets on the next.
- Similar detail across neighbours: nothing far more detailed than what sits beside it.

When something looks like it clashes, check it against these three first.
