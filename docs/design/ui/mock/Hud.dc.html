Money sits beside the finds, top left: the bank's sun coin and a gilt total. By default it shows around a bank and when landed or paused, because the total only changes at a bank; Always keeps it up.

Bank to money. The coins leave the pennant, climb the left edge to the chip and the total ticks as each lands, with the sum beside it for a moment. The path stays in the upper left; nothing crosses the plane.

Charge. Five cells, each half a second of surge (a full meter is 2.5 s). The chevrons light whenever there is any charge: surge is there to hold. The roll mark lights only when all five are full. Holding surge drains the cells from the right; the roll turns them white, spins its mark and spends the lot.

Speed joins the charge in one plate, in km/h as the logbook already uses. It turns gold above boost speed (208). When it matters shows it only while the speed is changing fast or is above boost.

Extras. Flight best marks a bank that beats this flight's best line. Flight earned shows what this flight has paid beside the total.
