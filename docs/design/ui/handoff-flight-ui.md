# Flight UI: handoff (loop `flight-ui`, 2026-11-11)

Built from `spec-flight-ui.md` and the mock-ups in `mock/`. The trail is
`git log --grep='^ITER(flight-ui)'`; each commit carries its decisions,
checks and stills.

## What's built

A launch with no flags now gives:

- **Title** (`scripts/endless/ui/title_menu.gd`): the name top left, a slip
  with Start, Settings, Credits, Quit. The stage, the parked plane and the
  takeoff are as before.
- **Pause** (`pause_menu.gd`): Esc or the pad's Start in flight; the tree
  is paused under it. Resume, Logbook, Photo mode, Controls, Settings, Quit
  to title (reloads the scene, about 30 s), Quit.
- **Settings** (`settings_page.gd`, `game_settings.gd`): Preset, 3D
  resolution, Display, VSync; Master, Effects, Ambience; Mouse sensitivity,
  Invert pitch, HUD hints. Each row applies at once and is saved
  (`user://look.cfg`, `sound.cfg`, new `settings.cfg`). A preset change
  restarts the flight when the page closes.
- **Controls** and **Credits** (`controls_page.gd`, `credits_page.gd`):
  bindings read from the InputMap; credits from
  `assets/audio/ATTRIBUTION.md`, then Godot's licence and notices.
- **Flight HUD** (`flight_hud.gd`): finds chip, region tag, money chip,
  charge cells with the speed, a hint that fades after takeoff, F2/F3 notes
  on a chip.
- **Chain HUD** (`scripts/endless/near_miss_hud_plate.gd`): the plate by
  the plane, its strip, the bank and pennant, lost, flight best.
- **Coin flight** (`coin_flight.gd`): a bank's coins fly from the pennant
  to the money chip and tick the total. Real payouts, most banks +1.
- **Glyphs** (`scripts/endless/near_glyphs.gd`, data generated by
  `tools/glyph_data.py`): the new set in colour, no sun.
- **The kit** all of it draws with: `scripts/endless/ui/ui_kit.gd`.

## Where to play it

`.\run-endless-flight.cmd`, and `.\run-endless-flight.cmd --ui=old` for
today's UI beside it.

Flags: `--ui=old` (all of the old UI); finer: `--menus=old`, `--hud=old`,
`--near-hud=ring`, `--glyphs=old|gilt`; the corner HUD's
`--hud-money=always`, `--hud-speed=always|off`, `--hud-earned`,
`--hud-hints=off`; the chain's `--chain-timer=fuse`, `--chain-best=0`;
`--open-settings`; `--settings-dir=DIR` for throwaway settings files.

## What's stood in or left

- The charge plate's two small marks are the mock-up's chevrons and round
  arrow: the `surge` and `roll` glyphs are both a ring round a speck at that
  size. `roll` is used on the larger notice by the plane; `surge` is unused.
- Glyph ids with no place yet: `landing`, `line`, `event`, `plane`, the ten
  place glyphs, the alternates.
- The credits read "A game by [YOUR NAME]" (`GAME_BY` in `menu_base.gd`);
  the name on the title is `GAME_NAME` there.
- The logbook, shop, landing card and photo mode keep their old look.
- Menu labels are set in Barlow Semi Condensed; the mock-up as the user saw
  it showed them in the system face (a styling slip). His choice is open.
- The fonts' OFL files are not in `export_presets.cfg`'s include filter, so
  an export's credits skip them.
- The launchers pass `--windowed`, so a saved Fullscreen shows only in a
  launch without that flag.

## What's open

- Payouts: the coin beat is one coin for most banks. Rescaling payouts and
  shop prices together was put to the user; no answer yet.
- Never checked: a real gamepad (events only), the stick on the menus, the
  mouse on the title and credits, the hint on a second takeoff, a bank as
  the plane lands, the logbook or shop opened with coins in the air,
  `--open-settings` over a chain, fullscreen and sizes other than 1440x900
  and 1920x1080, dark forest from low down, an exported build, frame time.
- One unexplained failure: a no-flag smoke in the menus lane failed once
  (67+ errors, text not captured) and passed on every run since.
