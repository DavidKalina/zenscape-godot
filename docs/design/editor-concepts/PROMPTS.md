# Editor UI concept prompts

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. Concept mockups, not implemented game screenshots.

Shared brief: High-fidelity desktop Zenscape game UI over a low-poly alpine landscape. Hybrid miniature placement for Bigfoot, arches, rocks and logs, with planting and Grow controls for foliage. Tactile, playful, restrained interface, clear typography, visible active interactions, no property inspector. Landscape occupies most of the screen.

## miniature-shelf

Direction A: MINIATURE SHELF. Contemporary warm ivory and moss-green interface, rounded soft beveled surfaces, compact UI. Bottom shallow ivory display shelf contains beautifully lit 3D miniature Bigfoot, Arch, Rock, Log on individual tiny plinths; Bigfoot selected and slightly lifted, friendly brown shaggy cryptid with big feet. Along shelf upper edge tabs 'Miniatures' and 'Grow'. Right end of shelf a small integrated growth tray shows Pine, Meadow seed packets and a green 'Grow' button. A larger carried Bigfoot hovers a little above a valid circular landing shadow in the scene, connected only by composition to its shelf position, near a tree; tiny tooltip 'Drop to place'. Top left small 'zenscape', top right compact undo redo and sun buttons. Every object reads as an actual toy you can grab. Clean restrained design, not excessive wood or fantasy trim.

## field-kit

Direction B: FIELD KIT. Elegant botanical field-kit interface with light warm paper, dark evergreen lettering and muted apricot highlights. Bottom left a tidy shallow seed box, three illustrated paper packets labeled 'Pines', 'Meadow', 'Ferns'; middle compact controls labeled 'Brush size' with three dot sizes and prominent 'Grow' button. Bottom right an open slim wooden specimen tray with four actual 3D collectible miniatures: Bigfoot, stone arch, rock, log; no cards around miniatures. Main active interaction: cursor scattering seeds along a curved terrain stroke, visible succession of tiny sprouts into young pines and full pines, restrained sparkling pollen and a soft dotted brush footprint. Make the stages spatially readable in one screenshot. Upper UI extremely sparse. Sophisticated cozy nature toy, not parchment RPG. Palette uses about bottom 20% of image.

## floating-nursery

Direction C: FLOATING NURSERY. Sleek airy minimal overlay UI with translucent pale cream floating pill-shaped controls, dark pine-green icons and coral selected highlights. Distinct layout: compact vertical miniature shelf on LEFT with 3D Bigfoot, arch, rock, log visibly protruding slightly beyond soft square wells. Bottom CENTER small horizontal foliage dock with miniature pine seedling, fern, flowers, plus 'Grow' and a compact growth progress dial labeled 'Young' and 'Mature'. Scene is large and panoramic. Selected small grove in world has a subtle circular boundary and three lightweight contextual circular handles for move rotate scale; nearby tiny contextual pill 'Grow grove'. Grove displays charming pines at various ages, one tree shown mid-unfurl with restrained leaf motion marks. Top left small 'zenscape', top right undo redo sun icons. Expressive premium game UI, actual implementable interface, not generic SaaS dashboard.
