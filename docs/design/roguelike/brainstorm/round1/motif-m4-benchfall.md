# Benchfall — "Every ledge was cut by a man standing on the one above"
Lenses: verb = fall / descend; purpose = quarry; decay = half-collapsed; mood = oppressive; shape in a layer = a tall stack (isles over isles).

Pitch: You arrive on the cap bench of an open quarry pit and the floor of it is not there: four crescent benches spiral down round a hole, each overhanging the next, the lowest 60 m under your boots in blue shade. Going down is the easy part and the whole game of it, because every miss lands on a bench below and the pit forgives you; what it does not forgive is how deep, dark and near the thing at the bottom you get.

## Side (north-south cut through the pit; 1 row ~ 4 m)
```

    ||             keel of 1 overhangs the pit
```

Each bench is its own isle (turf top, rock keel). Benches are placed so bench N+1 lies half under the gap between bench N's crescent ends and half under its keel: step off anywhere on the pit side and you land on N+1 within a pocket sail. Footprint about 64 x 64 m, height span about 60 m.

## Play
1. **Arrive on the cap (rim N).** Turf, a chewed lip, spoil slabs along the pit edge. Binoculars show all four benches and the floor arena, so the descent is readable before it begins.
2. **Down by walking.** A grand stair switchbacks down the cap's inner wall and ends in a landing that stops short (the next flight fell): a 6 m drop onto the east bench's rubble heap. Run, drop, roll; the slabs underfoot start to grind (voussoirs, below).
3. **Down by falling on purpose.** Run off the cap's lip and pocket-sail across the pit: a 14 m drop glides about 50 m (3.7 across per 1 down), enough to skip the east bench and reach the west bench direct. The greedy line. The flow-charge slam is the landing brake on a narrow bench, or you skate off its far lip to the next one down.
4. **Down by sliding.** The east bench carries a spoil chute (a balustrade slide, the old stone-sled way) down the pit wall to the west bench: fast and committed, ends on a lip you twirl off.
5. **The hoist.** The west bench holds a tower shell, the quarry's lift well: a wall-kick chimney going back up toward the cap. So the pit is not one-way. A layer can put its exit at the top (rim N in, rim E mid, back up to leave) or at the bottom (rim S).
6. **The floor.** The lowest bench is the quarry floor, an arena round a drum the masons stopped on. A headless body paces it. Rim S is the exit and the only edge over true void.
Moves named: run, pocket sail, flow-charge slam, slide, wall kick, ledge grab and chain, twirl.

## Threat
- **Spoil slabs** (`trap_voussoirs`) on the pit lips and the chute head: slabs that fall a beat after the pilot lands. Over the bench below it costs 2; on the floor bench's lip, over the void, 3. Tell is the trap's own (slabs a few cm off true, a grind on first touch).
- **The headless body** on the floor bench, pacing the arena (a knock-off duel on a bench with a void lip). Also a tool: knock it off the east bench and it lands on the bench below and sits there as a wall to climb or a plug in the chute; its fall is the sound tell for the next pilot.
- Fallen slabs land as rubble heaps on the bench below: steps for the 1.6 m SET_OFF climb toward the tower.
- Fail ladder: three of the four benches fail to the next bench down. Only the floor is void. Generator rule: +1 cost on the floor bench and the cap's outer lip.

## Combos
Chains here are the stack's own: every bench catches whatever falls from the one above and throws it on to the next.
1. **By accident: the slab cascade.** Standing on the cap's pit lip as it goes (voussoirs), you drop 14 m onto bench 2 and land tumbling (a fall stun); you slide into the spoil chute's head, which carries a downed body as it carries a standing one; the chute spills you (toss, still stunned) onto bench 3's lip; you roll off it and drop to bench 4, landing laid out in the headless body's patrol. Four links, three tiers, no input: the cost is position (you are now on the floor bench with the thing that paces it).
2. **On purpose: the skip.** Sprint off the cap, pocket sail across the pit (about 50 m for 14 m down), twirl-land on bench 3's near lip so the chimney is under your hands, wall-kick the hoist up to the cap's rim. A descent, a rise, a rim to rim, all in the stack's own column. The skilled line is also the one that avoids the headless body.
3. **Ridden on the body: the shoved giant.** Bait the headless body to the east bench's lip, drop onto its back from the cap with a slam (the flow charge's downdraft) so it is knocked off; it falls and lands on bench 3, and you ride its fall as a lift down. It is a physical thing for the generator to carry: the knocked body becomes a block on the bench below that raises the 1.6 m SET_OFF to the tower's mouth.
**Ports.** Catches from outside: a body thrown in from a neighbour above or beside lands on the cap or a bench (any airborne body falls through the pit, the stack is a net four tiers deep, so an over-thrown cannon shot or the dragon's swipe is caught by bench 2 or 3 instead of the void); a rider off the sky ray can be dropped onto the cap. Throws out: the spoil chute throws a pilot (still stunned if he was) out of rim E's adit mouth toward the east, 14 m under the cap, so a neighbour at that tier can aim a catcher (a seesaw, a head, an updraft) at rim E; rim S throws toward the south and down, with the floor bench's lip as a launch sill (a cannon or a head there would fire him on to the next layer's isle).

## Mystery
The stone for the great heads was cut here, bench by bench from the top down. The drum on the floor is not a drum but a half-freed figure, and the headless body keeps circling it, lost and head-hunting; the inhaler's head hovers elsewhere because it was finished first. Told with debris only: wedge-holes in rows, a drum lying ready to roll, rope-scars on the lips, rune grooves ending mid-line on the cut faces.

## Family knobs
- `benches`: 3 / 4 / 5 (stack height about 46 to 78 m)
- `turn`: clockwise / counter (built in: the generator never turns isles)
- `wreck`: 0..1, how much of each crescent has fallen (broken landings, gaps, slab count)
- `hoist`: tower on bench 3 or bench 2, or none; `chute`: on / off

## Sockets
- rim: N on the cap facing out (arrive), E on bench 2 (the adit mouth, a side door), S on the floor bench (exit, over the void)
- arena: the floor bench (the headless body's); a small one on the cap
- cover: under the cap's keel overhang and inside the adit, plenty of it
- perch: the cap by the derrick, the west bench by the tower (a trader in the shade)

## Generator fit
Role: hero (or arena when the headless body is the layer's duel). Footprint about 64 x 64 m, height span about 60 m. Its rims sit at three heights, so neighbours can be above and below it at once: the first isle that holds a descent. It lets the generator make a "descent layer" with arrival high and exit low.

## Distinct from
`stair_clouds`: one stepped mesa climbed by an 8-flight stair; this is four isles hung under one another, descended mostly by falling, ruin cut down rather than built up. `hollow_keel`: the only one that hangs under, but one plate over one hall flown or walked through; this is a stack with an open pit through it, the undersides being the next level's ceiling.
