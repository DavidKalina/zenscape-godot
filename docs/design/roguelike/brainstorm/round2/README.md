## The place, first

- **The Rootbound Cut** is the stronger *place*: mostly land (grass and fern
  on the ribs), a silhouette that reads from the arrival rim, collapse that
  is uneven (the north comb crisp, the south caved to a root ravine) and a
  story in the stone (tally marks, a head still joined at the nape). Risk:
  parallel trenches are a grid by nature; the place stage must bend and
  vary them (`ribs`, `caved` knobs) so it reads as a cut hill, not a comb of
  boxes. At 170 x 60 m it is the largest footprint in the pool.
- **The Long Court** is the stronger *threat*: two hunters on an open floor
  between walls three times his height. Its place is one strong image (a
  black slot in a mesa, a square of sky at the end), but it is more stone
  than land, and Olmec basalt walls are the style the user called "a
  different game" when stacked; the place stage must bury and weather it.
