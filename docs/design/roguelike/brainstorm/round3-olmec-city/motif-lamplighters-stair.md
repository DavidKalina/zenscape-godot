# The Lamplighters' Stair — "They lit it every night for a climber who never came. Light it now and see who comes instead."
Lenses: climb · light as a price · the ruin still doing its old job · a broken hillside · enemy as tool
Slug: `lamplighters_stair`

Pitch: You come in at the foot of a dark turf hillside that climbs away
into the haze, and up it goes a processional stair in three long runs,
switching back and forth, each landing marked by a small old lamp still
burning: a lit zigzag going up. Between the lamps the hill is black. Every
dead cresset and beacon on it is yours to light, and every one you light
is a call to the one who walks the upper landing.

Place: One great broken hillside isle of turf over the void, rising north
in three uneven shelves (the foot meadow, the lower shelf, the upper shelf)
to a ragged summit 43 m over the foot. Its scarps are not one wall: on the
west they come apart into ragged steps of earth and rock (the dark way);
in the middle they stand sheer; on the east the lower scarp is faced with
a basalt revetment, the **fuse wall**, its holds a diagonal run of corbels
with a dead oil runnel climbing beside them. The stair (the city's epic
stair, three flights a run, serpent cheeks) leans free off the foot
meadow against the first scarp, then cuts back west into the hill, then
east again: a Z laid on the hillside. Off the lower shelf's west tongue,
over the void, a lone rock holds the **wick spire**; on the lower shelf's
west edge a **votive pier** stands too tall for him, its lamp shelves
zigzagging to a dark alcove. On the upper shelf: the **hush gallery** in
the west, dug against the summit scarp, and in the east the **drum
terrace**, a court of loose slabs under a sheer rock shelf, a great cresset
dead at its corner. The arrival view (from the foot meadow's south rim,
off the stair's axis, south-west of it): the eye goes up stair 1's lamps,
across the dark to stair 2's, back to stair 3's and the summit's beacon,
with the votive pier and the spire as dark verticals on the left against
the haze, and far off on the left, a small lit isle where something stone
walks. The stone is the Olmec's weathered basalt; the land is turf, worn
paths, earth hanging from the shelves' lips.

Play: three ways up, and at every shelf a choice between lit and fast,
or dark and slow.
- **The processional** (exposed, fast, the rune line): run stair 1 (no
  one on it). On the lower shelf, stair 2 (its first landing a fault).
  On the upper shelf, the Keeper's beat crosses stair 3's foot: wait in
  the dark for his back, or **light the fuse** (below) and slip up stair 3
  while he walks east to the fire. About 60-90 s.
- **The dark way** (safe, slow, poor): the hill's west edge, ragged earth
  steps (a set-off each, some narrow, over the void), up past the votive
  pier (a refuge), along the lower shelf's west tongue, up the next ragged
  scarp, through or past the hush gallery (the refuge on the upper shelf),
  up the long goat climb to the summit's west end, then along the summit
  to the rim out. Off his lanes but for one crossing. 3+ minutes.
- **The drum shortcut** (clever, exposed, the rare): from the lower
  shelf's east end, climb the fuse wall's corbels (in the dark, by torch;
  or light its runnel first and the flame lights each hold as it climbs,
  then lights the great cresset at the top: the Keeper comes). On the drum
  terrace, let him come for you across the slabs: when he pounces and
  lands, the slab under you hops; jump as it hops and it launches you onto
  the drum shelf (a rare), whose steps go up to the summit by the rim out,
  skipping stair 3.
- At the lower shelf you can also climb the **beacon tower** (its giant
  steps, its shelf level with the upper shelf: a hop across). Fast, but
  your torch lights its crown and calls him to the spot you step off at.

Threat: **the Keeper**, a whole Olmec (headless body with its head on,
chase on) walking the upper shelf between stair 2's summit and the drum
terrace, across stair 3's foot. He hears and sees; light shows you to
him. Every fire that is lit calls every Olmec within its reach (a beacon).
His swat knocks 4.5 m: on the shelves' void edges, on the stair cheeks, on
the corbels, that is a fall. The fault landing on stair 2 drops him a
course into its chamber if he chases you down it. **Far off**, on a small
lit isle west, a headless body roams, a silhouette against its tower's
light. As tools: the fuse is a lure on a delay (he walks east for 15-20 s
while you climb west); the drum court turns his landing into your launch;
the fault turns his weight into an open chamber.

Could combo (hunches, nothing sequenced): his swat on the drum terrace
throwing you back over the fuse wall's lip onto the corbels (or past
them: 14 m, a heart); the fault breaking under him as he chases you down
stair 2, the chamber's rare suddenly open; a lit great cresset barged over
on the drum terrace leaving a spill on the slabs he lands on; the spire's
crown lit calling him to the lower shelf's west tongue edge while you are
on the spire over the void. Ports: catches a body thrown in from a
neighbour on the wide foot meadow; throws out off every shelf edge into
the void.

Mystery: The stair was a procession, lit every night lamp by lamp by the
lamplighters, for a climber who came up out of the dark. The little lamps
still burn; the great fires don't. The Keeper walks the upper landing as
the lamplighters' last one did, waiting for a light to tend.

**The land.** One KitLand island (about 165 x 215 m) with three shelves
14.4 m apart (foot 0, lower 14.4, upper 28.8, summit 43.2, the summit
rising 2.4 m north). Each scarp is sheer rock over a turf talus (3-8 m,
so the rock is 6-11 m: a fall off it is free or one heart), wandering;
west of a wandering line it comes apart into nine ragged set-off steps (the
dark way). Turf swells (±1.3 m) on the shelves, held flat round what
stands. The foot meadow has a bay cut out of its east side (the void
reads from the arrival) and a west shoulder. Two more KitLand islands:
the spire's rock (a 2 m gap off the lower shelf's west tongue, 0.8 m
lower) and the far isle (170 m west, lit tower, a headless body roaming).
