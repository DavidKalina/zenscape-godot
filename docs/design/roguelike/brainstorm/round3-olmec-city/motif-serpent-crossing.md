# The Serpent Crossing — "It still keeps its timetable. Nobody has waited for it in a thousand years."
Lenses: ride · colossal · spread wide over a void · sacred · the ruin still doing its old job (the serpent still runs its line)
Slug: `serpent_crossing` (from city-serpent-round1.md: the rune serpent as the city's train)

Pitch: You come in on a low grass isle at the edge of a wide dark gulf. To
the right a canopied dais stands out over the drop, its eight tick stones
dead; far ahead a column wound with a spiral of grooves climbs out of the
haze to a high green isle, and somewhere out in the dark a line of gold
moves, turns, and comes back toward you.

Place: A low turf isle (A, 0 m), broad and uneven, its west side stepping up
in two grassy terraces, a tongue of land running north into the gulf. On its
east cliff the **waiting dais** (`kukul_dais`) juts out over the void like a
balcony, its trough open on the far side: the serpent's line runs past it.
Under the dais, a long drop down, a sunk **court of ash** (K, -9.6 m;
`colossus_ashbowl`) with giant steps climbing its south side back to A. North
of the dais, standing alone in the gulf on its own paved pillar (R), the
**threading ring** (`kukul_ring`). Far north, rising from the haze, the
**coil** (`kukul_coil`), the serpent's spiral ramp round a column, its cap the
highest stone in sight. To its west the **far isle** (F, 32 m), a high turf
plateau whose east cliff is worn concave where the serpent has brushed it for
a thousand years. Between A and F, on the west, the slow way: the **wedged
chunk** (`city_wedge`) lies across A's tongue up to a turfed basalt pillar
(B, 9.6 m), the **leaper's pit** (`city_pit`) crosses from B to the
**wrapped pillar** (`city_wrap`), a pillar-isle of its own whose ramp winds up
to F's edge. Everything stands over an open void; far pillars with lit
crowns hang in the haze all round.

View on arrival (from A's south rim, looking north): the wedge's ramp up to
B, the wrap's square tower beyond it, F's green lip high above, the coil
column to the right of it with its rune spiral, and the ring standing
nearer on the right. The eye goes dais → ring → coil → F.

Play:
- **The slow way (safe, poor):** walk A's tongue to the wedge, up its slab
  (his ramp) or its chimney (yours) to B. Cross the pit by the inner cornice
  under the plinth tops (the bare moveset), or sail it (ability shortcut).
  Climb the wrap: its ramp (his lane, long, exposed) or the recess up the
  core's south face, with the safe cell halfway. Step off the ramp's north
  corner onto F. The whole Olmec keeps this side: he walks B, jumps the pit
  and walks the wrap.
- **The ride (fast, rich, exposed):** wait on the dais while the tick stones
  light one a second. Board at a run off its lip into the saddle (miss and you
  fall into the ash: caught, but the lap is gone). Ride through the ring, out
  over the gulf, round and up the coil beside the ramp. **Get off at the right
  moment:** the saddle passes F's worn cliff a step above F's ground for a
  couple of seconds; jump out onto F. Stay on and the collar shrugs you onto
  the coil's cap (a rare), from where the only way on is to walk down the ramp
  and board again as the next lap comes round, then jump off at F.
- **The bail:** jump off as the serpent goes over R, the ring's pillar, and
  climb the ring's crown courses to its cap (a rare). The way off R is back
  onto the serpent: drop from the cap or an abutment onto its back as it
  threads under you.
- The choice: walk with the Olmec, or wait for the serpent and trust a jump
  over the void, twice.

Threat: the void (the serpent's line is over it everywhere); the serpent's
wake (a body in its girth not riding is tossed out of its way: off the
wrap's corner, off R); the shrug; the whole Olmec on the slow side (a swat
there knocks you 4.5 m, off B or the pit's plinths into the dark).
Also a tool: the serpent is the ride (rule 1). The Olmec is lured by the
unlit great cresset on A's west terrace (light it to pull him down off B).

Could combo: the Olmec on the wrap's ramp while the serpent climbs the coil
beside it; a wake toss from the wrap's NE corner lands you on the serpent's
back or in the dark. A swat off the dais drops you into the ash. Ports: the
dais and A's east lip catch anything thrown east onto the line; F's rim
throws out north.

Mystery: the dais's calendar and tick stones; the serpent still keeps a
timetable for a city with no one in it. The ash under the dais: a court built
to catch people who missed.

Kit used: kukul_dais, kukul_collar, kukul_ring, kukul_coil, colossus_ashbowl,
city_wedge, city_pit, city_wrap, city_pillar, cresset and beacon tower
(CityFires), KitLand islands (A, F), turf caps (B), the whole Olmec
(headless body + inhaler head), the rune serpent.

Kit asks: the shrug thrown outward (off the coil into the dark, a toss to
tech) as an option; the Olmec's body reading the ash (soft ground) so he
can be dropped into it.

Family knobs: serpent pace (the ride's length), coil turns, which side the
Olmec starts (B or the wrap).

Sockets: rim in A south (face +z); rim out F north (face -z). Arena: A's
terrace, B's crown. Cover: the dais shrine, the wrap's cell. Perch: the
ring's cap, the coil's cap, F's summit.

Generator fit: hero; footprint about 190 x 220 m, height -70 to 40; a sprawl
layer's landmark, the climb's big step.

Distinct from: the stacked court (a stack climbed on foot); this is a
crossing, and its fastest way is a moving enemy.
