# The Felled Colonnade — "They set their columns at the edge, so that one day someone would knock them over."
Lenses: enemy as tool · a broken pair (two isles that were one) · the ruin still doing its old job (the columns were always the bridges)
Slug: `felled_colonnade` (seed: the user's own rule-1 example, "lure the Olmec into a pillar so it falls and becomes a platform")

Pitch: You arrive on a low turf forecourt and the city is broken in two
in front of you. A gap too wide to jump runs north between two isles, and
on your side one great drum column still stands at its very edge, with the
stubs of its fallen row behind it. The Olmec walks among them. Across the
gap, two pale bed-stones wait on the far rim, square to the column. Far
off, the other isle climbs to a lit beacon.

Place: Two isles of turf over the void, split long ago down a north-south
fault, about 12 to 13 m apart all along it. **The near isle** (the
arrival, west) is three heights of grass: the **forecourt** (0 m) where
you land, the **colonnade terrace** (4.8 m) above its north cliff, where
the last standing column (`maker_felled`) stands by the fault with a row
of broken stubs behind it, and the **gallery** (11.6 m), a high grassy
rise along the north whose south cliff juts out in a bastion beside the
column's fall line. **The far isle** (east) climbs: **shelf A** (4.8 m)
is a promontory straight across from the column, shut at its north by a
24 m gate wall with a keystone plug (`colossus_plug`); behind the gate a
grass ramp climbs to the **upper walk** (11.6 m), a long turf bench; from
it the **epic stair** (`city_stair`, 14.4 m) climbs north to the
**crown** (26.0 m), where a beacon burns and an Olmec head waits. South
of the far isle, a **pier** reaches back toward the arrival; a
four-span **causeway** (`city_causeway`) joins it to the forecourt, and the
**mouse run** (`city_mouserun`) pierces the 25 m wall that cuts the pier
from the far isle's **east terraces**, which step up to the upper walk.
On the gallery's east edge, opposite the upper walk, the makers' half-done
**chocked slab** (`maker_chock`) stands on end, roped back.
The view on arrival: from the forecourt's south edge you look north-east;
the eye goes column → the bed-stones across the gap → the gate wall
behind them → the stair → the beacon. The causeway leaves your right hand
for the pier, a long way round. The stone is the Olmec's weathered basalt; the
ground is grass, and fallen drums lie in it.

Play: three ways from the forecourt to the crown, and the choice is the
column.
- **The bridge** (clever, exposed, fastest). Climb to the terrace by the
  **east steps** right by the gap (the void at your shoulder), get
  between the column and the gap, and let the Olmec see you. He charges
  from the west, meets the column, and it goes over **east**: a bridge
  onto shelf A, the bed-stones under its end. Don't be under it (it
  knocks you 7 m sideways along the rim). Run the drums, drop onto shelf
  A: he comes over after you. Stand at the plug's face and slip aside as
  he pounces: the keystone breaks and you run the gate ramp to the upper
  walk, him on your heels.
- **The ramp** (the column the wrong way). If he hits it from the gap
  side (you were west of it, he came after you up the east steps, or his
  landing went off east of it), it falls **west** along the terrace: the
  top drums roll, and the rest lies as a 5.5 m wall-walk beside the
  gallery's bastion. Climb the plinth, the stub and the spall onto the
  drums, then hop up onto the gallery (1.2 m). The gallery runs north to
  the **chocked slab**: climb the striker's block, slam the plate, and the
  slab swings down across the gap onto the upper walk. Not a dead end:
  a middle route that skips the gate and the ramp.
- **The long way round** (safe, slow, poor). From the forecourt's
  south-east corner walk the causeway (106 m) to the pier, through the
  mouse run (the Olmec can't follow), up the east terraces (seven 1.6 m
  set-offs) to the upper walk. Few runes, no rare.
All three end on the epic stair under the crown's head.

Threat: **the Feller**, a whole Olmec (headless body wearing its head,
chase on, chase routes on) on the colonnade terrace, walking a beat west
of the column (STAND-IN beat, the Stacked Court's Keeper). He hunts by
sight and sound; his swat is 1 heart and a 4.5 m knock, and the terrace's
east edge is the void. **The column**, falling, knocks you; its rolling
drums (a wrong-way fall) knock you. **The chocked slab** knocks whoever
stands on the far socket. **The drum floor** on the forecourt: when he
comes down off the terrace at you, his landing hops the slabs and throws
you, or, jumped in time, launches you 5.6 m. **The head** on the crown
looks down the stair.
As tools: the column (bridge or ramp), the plug (only he breaks it), the
drum floor (his landing launches you to the rare stump), the cresset
south of the drum floor (light it, and he comes down off the terrace
across the floor to it).

Could combo: his pounce on the drum floor launching you while he is still
stunned below; the column's fall knocking you along the rim into his
reach; the head's blast from the crown throwing you down the stair onto
the upper walk, onto the chocked slab's socket as it falls. Ports: catches
a body thrown in onto the forecourt's wide turf; throws out off the crown
and off every rim into the void.

Mystery: The makers built the city whole, then split it themselves: they
set columns on the brink and chocked slabs at the gap, bridges waiting
for a push. The bed-stones on the far rim were cut for this column. Most
of the row fell long ago, and only the stubs are left. The Feller walks
among the stubs as if guarding the last one.
