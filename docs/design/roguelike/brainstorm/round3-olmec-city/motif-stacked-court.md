# The Stacked Court — "Every crown was lit for someone climbing. Someone still keeps the well."
Lenses: climb · colossal · a tall stack (isles over isles) · sacred · the ruin still doing its old job (its fires still burn)
Slug: `stacked_court` (from the k7 hunch, kit-k7-vertical.md: "The Stacked Court")

Pitch: You come in low, on a turf forecourt at the foot of the city, and
everything above you is lit: a serpent stair climbs to a rim of lamps, two
stepped pillars stand behind it joined by a bridge of fire bowls, a summit
tower burns over them, and far back a needle of stone holds a light higher
than all of it. Further out, pillars at other heights fade into the brown
haze with their own lights, layer over layer, and you count them by their
fires.

Place: A stack of basalt pillar-isles rising out of an unseen floor, each
layer a course-set higher and further back, round a court of air. The
**forecourt** (F, crown 0 m) is the foot: a broad hex column wearing a ragged
turf cap that hangs over its edges, its west side climbing in two grassy
terraces. From its north edge the **serpent stair** (the kit's epic stair,
three flights, 14.4 m) climbs to the **well** (M, crown 14.4 m), a great
pillar whose crown is a ring round a round well with the **drum stair**
wound down inside it to a core floor and a cold great cresset. Set face to
face against the well's two back faces stand the **west and east pillars**
(U and T, crowns 38.4 m), stepped down in five collar courses to the well's
crown, and between their crowns runs the **causeway** behind the well, its
two middle piers standing on the well's ring, a lamp on its parapets over
each pier. T bears the **summit tower** (a beacon tower to 52.8 m, lit).
Far behind, the **needle** (crown 115 m) carries a lit tower: the layer
above. The view on arrival: from the forecourt's south edge you look up the
stair; the eye goes stair → the lamps on the well's rim → the causeway's
lamps between the stepped pillars → the summit tower → the needle, each
light higher and further. The stone is the Olmec's weathered basalt
(organ-pipe flutes on every shaft, glyph bands on every collar); growth has
taken every walked top (grass on the crowns, collars and the deck; turf
caps hanging off the high crowns). Broken unevenly: the stair's first
landing is a fault over a chamber, the causeway's middle bay is cracked
over the Keeper's beat (pre-cut, still standing), a column lies in drums on the forecourt.

Play: two ways up from the forecourt to T's far edge, and the well between.
- **The court** (his lane, open, about 78 s). Run the serpent stair (or
  crouch on a cheek crest and slide back down it). On the well's rim, go
  west round the ring, past the well's mouth (look down the coil) and the
  south-west cresset, step across the seam onto U's lowest collar and climb
  its prism chain (prism, prism, lip, five times) to U's crown. Then the
  causeway: 79 m east between lit parapets, over the cracked bay and the
  Keeper below it, into the summit head's sight at its far end; round the
  summit tower to the rim out.
- **The tower** (walker's, a climb, about 70 s). Climb F's beacon tower by
  its giant steps (your torch lights its crown as you reach it, and every
  Olmec near turns to the light); from its shelf hop the seam onto the
  well's rim, go east round the ring and climb T's collar chain straight to
  the summit crown and the rim out. Shorter, but the fire you lit is
  bringing the Keeper.
- **The well** is the way down and the Keeper's home: drop off any drum
  turn's inside onto the turn below, standing; at the core a great cresset
  waits to be lit.
You choose between the open stair and causeway (his lane, the head's sight)
and the tower and collars (a climb, a light that calls him).

Threat: **the Keeper**, a whole Olmec (the headless body with its head
worn, chase on) walking the well's north ring under the causeway's bay,
his lanes the kit's (8.8 m): the stair, the ring, the drum's turns, the
deck; he climbs any flank collar to a crown. **The summit head**, an inhaler
head holding T's crown at the causeway's end, facing down the deck; its
headless body comes up the pillars' flanks (it came up M's east face in the
check). **The fires**: every unlit cresset (F's tower crown, the well's two
ring cressets, the core's great cresset, the forecourt's) calls every Olmec
within its reach when lit, and he barges a cresset over on his way (a lit
one spills burning on his lane). As tools: light F's tower and the Keeper
leaves the north ring for the forecourt corner while you climb T; light the
core cresset and he goes down into the well; lure him onto the bay and it
drops under him, 24 m, onto his own ring.

Could combo (hunches, nothing sequenced): the summit head's pull dragging a
walker along the deck onto the cracked bay just as the Keeper climbs onto it;
the Keeper's landing shock throwing a walker off the ring into the drum (the
turn below catches her); a gulp-and-blast from the head throwing her off T's
crown down onto the well's rim (24 m: a stun) or over the court; a barged
cresset's spill burning on the stair while he comes up it. Ports: catches a
body thrown in from a neighbouring isle on the forecourt's wide turf (soft);
throws out off T's far edge (the rim) and off every crown edge into the
haze.

Mystery: The fires were lit for climbers: a lamp at every rim, a bowl at
every pier, a tower on the summit, all still burning, though nobody comes.
The great cresset at the bottom of the well is the one that went out. The
Keeper walks the ring above it as if waiting for someone to light it again.

Kit used: `city_pillar` (F bare-crowned under turf, M with its well, U and
T collared, the needle), `city_drum_stair` (in M's well), `city_stair`
(fault on its first landing), `city_causeway` (bay in its middle span),
`ol_beacon` / CityFires `tower` (F's, T's summit, the needle's, the far
layers'), `maker_cresset` / CityFires `cresset` (great, cresset and crown
sizes), the breakables hub (`scripts/kit_isle/breakables.gd`), the headless
body and inhaler head (`scripts/enemies/`, the head's body through
BodySpawner), KitLand turf caps, GroundCover grass, GiantStone drums.

Kit asks:
- A door out of the drum stair's core (the kit's open question): with it
  the drum becomes a way up (F's foot → the core → the rim), as the hunch
  wants. Today the drum is a pit.
- A causeway that can rise (a sloped deck or a stair bay), so the top two
  need not stand level.
- A pillar `crown` option for a turf cap with a well (M's crown is paved).

Family knobs: `stack` (layers: 3 or 4 crowns, the needle on or off),
`bay` (which span cracks, or none), `lit` (which crowns burn: the more lit,
the fewer lures left), `keeper` (whole Olmec on the ring, or none).

Sockets: rims: in on F's south edge (facing south, 14 m run-up), out on T's
far edge (facing north-east). Arenas: the forecourt (12 m), the Keeper's
ring (4 m). Cover: under the causeway on the well's north ring (the deck
over you). Perches: the summit tower's shelf, U's crown.

Generator fit: role hero (the neighbourhood's landmark); footprint about
165 x 260 m (x -81 to 81, z -141 to 122); height span -70 to 130 m (the
shafts fade to -70; the needle's tower tops out at 130). Suits a tall
stack layer; its own far pillars stand only in the motif yard (a layer's
other isles are the depth).

Distinct from: the city kit gallery (the same pieces in a line: low C,
stair, causeway, A; here they stack round a court and each layer is higher
and further back), Sunken Giant (a climb up one body; here a city of
pillars with two ways up and a well), the Plumb Well (a scripted vertical
chain; here nothing is a chain).

**My read against the kit motif.** From the arrival the stack does read as

## Polish (round 1)

The user's read: "more architecture than land and very symmetric", and no
door out of the drum pillar. Worked in place in
`scripts/kit_isle/greybox/gb_stacked_court.gd`, plus a STAND-IN helper
`stacked_court_door.gd`.

**What changed.**
- **F is land, not a pillar.** The forecourt's hex column is gone. F and a
  new west bank are now one KitLand island hanging on its own rock keel
  (45 m) beside the city's pillars. Its south, south-east and south-west
  faces keep the old hex lines (`tuck` 0), so the rims stand where they
  did with the same lips (IN 2.6 from the land's edge).
- **A hill west of the stair.** It rises toward M in broad tilted meadows
  broken by two scarps (a set-off each, the lines wandering), not stacked
  plates. It runs on as the west bank into the corner between M and U,
  banked against M's south-west flank up to about 10 m. The hill is the
  stair's west flank, so the stair's pyramid flanks are off and its east
  cheek stands free. The east forecourt swells gently and its north-east
  edge is broken a step down (1.2 m: a double jump back up). The bank's
  far west lip drops a set-off over the haze.
- **The drum is a way up.** A walker-high door (3.6 m, too low for the
  Keeper's 7.5 m) cuts through M's south-west flank. Its tunnel runs under
  turn 0 and comes out on the drum's second turn at 5.6 m. Climb the coil
  out of the dark onto M's south rim. A sunken way cuts through the hill
  to the door, with two lamps on its rim, two at the mouth, and a carved
  lintel and jambs. The kit's drum has no door, so
  `stacked_court_door.gd` builds M and the drum the way CityKit `pillar`
  and `drum_stair` do (the same planes, Piece and stone), with the tunnel
  split out of the blocks it crosses. **Kit ask:** a `door` opt on
  `pillar` and `drum_stair`. Then the helper goes.
- **Growth and wear.** A turf mantle grows over M's south-west crown and
  hangs over the door. U wears a hanging earth mantle (its south and west
  faces grow 6.5-9 m) and a mound on its crown, so it no longer twins T,
  which keeps its tower. The needle's cap grows 5 m. Five fallen crown
  blocks of city stone lie on the bank and on F's broken edge (collided,
  sunk in). The fallen drums moved off the hill.
- **Spots for the collectibles round.** These were new in this round.
  - `rune_line`: the stair, the worn way, the tunnel, the coil, M's west
    ring, the causeway and the lintel.
  - `rune_risk`: the cracked bay over the Keeper, F's broken shelf, the
    bank's west lip and the stair's west cheek crest.
  - `rare`: the fault's chamber (behind the break), the well's bottom by
    the cold great cresset, and the summit tower's crown.
