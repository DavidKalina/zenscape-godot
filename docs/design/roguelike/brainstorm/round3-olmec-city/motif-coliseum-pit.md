# The Coliseum Pit — "They played for the sky here once. The far stands kept their seats; the near ones went to the dark."
Lenses: colossal · broken (a plateau half fallen into the void) · the ruin still doing its old job (two keepers of the court) · enemy as tool
Slug: `coliseum_pit` (seed: the city's ballcourt coliseum, city-landmarks-round1.md `city_court`; breaks round 2's `maker_corbel`)

Pitch: You come out on a turf shelf level with the coliseum's top tier and
look down into it: a long stone alley sunk into a broken plateau, rings
hung over it in serpent jaws, its north end snapped off into the void.
Across the pit, the land climbs in grassy steps to a high rim where a
temple pyramid stands side-on against the haze, its shrine's comb on top.
Something stone walks the far tier. Something bigger stands in the alley.

Place: A plateau isle of turf over the void, broken in three. The
**east shelf** (you arrive on it, 14.4 m over the court floor) is a tongue
of turf flush with the east stands' promenade; its north end has gone, so
the stands' back and the court's foundation hang bare over the abyss
there. The **court** (`city_court`) is sunk into the plateau: its stands'
backs are buried in turf on both sides, so from any distance it reads as a
stone trench cut in the land, not a building on it. Its north end zone
breaks off into the void (the piece's own broken end) and a tumbled chunk
of it hangs a jump off the lip. South of the gate the land falls to the
**gate hollow** (court floor level), a turf bowl whose east side steps
down from the shelf in three broken terraces and whose south edge is the
void. The hollow's west side is a 15 m terrace wall, and the **corbel
gate** (`maker_corbel`) is bored into it, its cracked outer bay facing the
hollow. Over the wall the **west terrace** runs behind the west stands
(flush with their promenade) and climbs, in ragged set-off steps, the
**shoulder** up to the **high rim**: a wide turf plateau 27.2 m up where
the **temple pyramid** (`city_pyramid`) stands with its stair facing
south, its north cliff over the void. Out of the corbel's back a **hollow
way** winds along the island's south edge, a long stepped gully up to the
high rim by the pyramid's stair foot. The rim's north-east corner, over
the void bay north of the court, rises in three ragged rings to the way
out. Grass on every walked top, turf hanging over
every broken edge, the basalt weathered and grimed.

The view on arrival (from the shelf's east end, looking west): the court's
long trench in the middle ground, the rings and roots over its alley; on
the right its snapped end and the void, the high rim's north cliff across
it, the pyramid in profile on top; on the left the hollow and its terraces
falling away. The eye goes: the stone rings → the far tier and the stone
figure on it → the shoulder's steps → the pyramid and its comb.

Play: three ways from the shelf to the high rim's far end.
- **The hollow way** (safe, slow, poor; about 2.5 min). Off the shelf's
  south end down its three broken terraces (4.8 m drops, free) into the
  gate hollow, across it out of the court's gate line, through the corbel
  gate's hall, and up the long gully on the island's south edge: set-off
  step after step, the void at your left shoulder the whole way, out by
  the pyramid's stair foot. A few runes. Nobody sees you but from the
  alley, through the gate, for a few seconds.
- **The corbel** (clever, exposed, fast). Drop into the court (rows, the
  rim, the ledge, slide the talud) where the Player owns the alley. Let
  him see you, run out the gate to the corbel's mouth and stand under the
  cracked bay till he pounces; dodge late. His landing brings the bay
  in. Climb the heap, the broken east haunch's courses, onto the roof and
  the west terrace, then the shoulder. A rare sits on the broken stair's
  first top (behind the break). The spill knocks you if you're slow; he
  climbs after you.
- **The far tier** (clever, exposed, scenic). From the court floor up the
  west end zone's prisms to the rim, the rows, onto the promenade the
  Watcher walks, and off its back onto the west terrace. His swat knocks
  you 4.5 m: along the promenade's north end that is over the void. A
  light on the east promenade's cresset pulls him across the pit first.
  The rings' roots swing you along the west wall to the end zone
  (a rune run, in the Player's sight).
- The high rim: past the pyramid (its stair to the shrine, a true safe
  room), or straight on up three ringed steps to the way out on the
  north-east corner.

Threat: **the Player**, a whole Olmec (body with head worn) who walks the
alley and its end zones (a STAND-IN beat); **the Watcher**, another whole
Olmec on the west promenade, hunting from the tier and dropping into the
alley. Both carry the breakables hub (the corbel, the pyramid stair's
fault landing, the cressets). As tools: the Player's landing breaks the
corbel bay open (the shortcut and the rare); the court's unlit cressets
(the promenades' north corners) call either of them when your torch lights
one; the pyramid's fault landing drops whoever stands on it.

Could combo (hunches): the Watcher's swat throwing you off the promenade
into the alley at the Player's feet; the Player's shockwave at the corbel
mouth knocking you into the spill; a fall off the hollow way's lip
(the void) after a knock from the Player climbing after you. Ports:
catches a body thrown onto the shelf or the high rim's wide turf; throws
out off the snapped court end, the hollow way's lip and the rim out.

Mystery: The ballcourt was a ceremony for the sky: rings to catch a ball
that stood for the sun. When the plateau broke, the north end of the court
fell into the dark and took the sky with it. The two stone keepers still
play their parts: one walks the alley, one watches from the tier, and the
pyramid's shrine above still has a door only someone small fits.

Kit used: `city_court`, `city_pyramid` (its shrine and fault), 
`maker_corbel` (CityBreaks2), CityFires cressets (on the pieces), the
breakables hub, the headless body and inhaler head (whole), KitLand turf,
GroundCover grass, GiantStone drums, SwingRope roots (via the host).

Kit asks:

  so the buried side isn't stone under turf.
- A pyramid with a shorter stair (the epic stair stands out 38 m).
- A broken court rim (a collapsed stand section) as a piece option.

Family knobs: `collapse` (how much of the east shelf survives), `keepers`
(one or two whole Olmecs), `corbel` (cracked or whole), `pyramid_turn`.

Sockets: rims: in on the shelf's east end (facing east), out on the high
rim's north-east corner (facing north-north-east, over the void bay). Arenas: the gate hollow, the
court's alley, the high rim before the pyramid. Cover: the corbel's hall.
Perches: the pyramid's summit court, the west promenade.

Generator fit: role hero; footprint about 310 x 190 m; height span about
-60 to 65 m (the pyramid's comb). Suits a sprawl layer's landmark.

Distinct from: the Stacked Court (a stack of pillars round air, a climb up
a column; here one broken plateau with a sunk court, land first, crossed
east to west and up); the landmarks yard (the same three pieces on a
plaza; here they sit in the land, broken, and the routes come from it).

**The land.** Five KitLand islands. The plateau (one heightfield: the gate
hollow at the court's floor, the 15 m terrace wall the corbel is bored
into, the west terrace flush with the promenade at 14.4, the shoulder's
eight steps, alternately grabbable set-off cliffs and short turf ramps, the
high rim at 27.2, three ragged rings up to the way out at 32.0, the hollow
way along the south edge from 0 to 27.2 in 17 set-off steps, open to the
void on its outer side). The shelf (14.4, flush with the east promenade,
its north end gone). The court's bed (bare rock under the alley, so the
snapped north end reads as broken plateau, not a stone box). The fallen
chunk and the lone spire (the rares). The court is sunk: both stands'
backs are under turf. The pyramid's paving runs under a turf berm at its
edges. Fallen drums for cover. Lamps (still: they call no one) mark the
corbel, the hollow way and the way out.

**Routes**, walked rim in to rim out through the real input (stick and
jump; `scratch/coliseum_pit_routes.gd`, `--route=safe|tier|corbel`):
1. **The hollow way** (safe, slow, poor): 104-113 s, calm and live, haze
   and torch. The shelf's broken terraces (4.8 m drops), the hollow, the
   corbel's hall, 17 grab-climbs up the gully, the pyramid's stair foot,
   up the high rim to the way out. Nobody touched it.
2. **The far tier** (exposed): 75-100 s. Rows down into the alley, the
   north end zone's four prisms to the rim, the rows, the Watcher's
   promenade, the terrace, the shoulder. Live: the Watcher spotted the
   walker on the prisms, wound up and pounced on the promenade (missed),
   then chased onto the terrace; the Player fell into the court's shaft
   and climbed out of it. The driver slips off the 4th prism about half
   the time (no dodging, no timing); it goes again.
3. **The corbel** (the enemy-as-tool, exposed): 128-142 s live. Into the
   alley in front of the Player, spotted, out the gate to the corbel's
   mouth; he inhaled and spat the walker, then pounced: his landing's
   shock brought the bay in (`cause shock`, 7 of 9 lure runs; in the others he
   gulped and swatted the walker away from the mouth first). Then the
   heap, the seven broken treads, the roof (15.6), the terrace, the
   shoulder. He climbs the broken stair after you (`climb(H)` seen live).
   Driver times include ~20 s of waiting for the lure; the climb from the
   heap to the terrace takes 25 s.

**Threat.** The Player: a whole Olmec (body plus head) whose beat is the
alley's south half and the gate's end zone (the shaft splits the alley:
his planner can't pass it). The Watcher: a whole Olmec on the west
promenade's length. Both chase (`chase`, `chase_routes`), both carry the
breakables hub (the corbel, the pyramid stair's fault landing, the court's
two unlit cressets). Real: everything above. STAND-IN: `Keepers_STANDIN`
(the beat): once the Olmec has picked a way at a listen, or while he
roams, it hands his own planner the beat's next point (`_start_search`), so
his routes and leaps are his own. Without it the Watcher leapt off the
promenade within 10 s and the Player walked out of the court.

a plinth" note: the court reads as a trench cut in turf (its stands' backs
are buried, its snapped end is rock), the corbel is a gate in a cliff of
the plateau, and the pyramid's paving sinks under turf berms. The hollow

near.
