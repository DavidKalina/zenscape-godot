## Top 6 picks

Chosen for a rule you learn in one death, a cheap build, and a Rule 0 that
is a real tool with real risk.

1. **h1 The Sun-Keepers (M).** The brief's inverted coil-head, sharpened:
   your own torch is their floor. Dousing it over the void is the kill and
   the danger at once. The torch steal turns one into a beacon that drags
   the Olmec off you. It's the purest Lethal Company rule in the round.
2. **h2 The Second Walker (M).** You learn it in 8 seconds, it punishes the
   refuge-camping the Olmec allows, and broken floors kill it. That ties it
   to the breakable kit and to the Olmec's own breaks. Its footprint buffer
   makes h5 and h7 cheap later. In co-op it chases whoever leads.
3. **a5 The Earth Mouths (M).** It's the round's best co-op story, and the
   only clean way to move the Olmec somewhere else on the layer. Rule 0 is
   brutal: you can swap yourself out, but only while stunned.
4. **x1 The Tithe-Taker (M).** It keeps the user's greed seed but turns it
   inside out: it follows the pile, not you. Dropping your haul as bait
   and adding one rune to ring the Olmec to it is clever play that costs
   real runes. Take x1 over h5.
5. **x3 / h3 The Echo, as the Twin Jars (M).** Build one card from these
   two. A displaced sound with its force behind it makes a lure, a remote
   breaker, and a way to turn the Olmec's own slam into a shockwave
   elsewhere. It needs the wanderer-noise signal, which v1's "hunts by
   light and sound" needs anyway.
6. **a4 The Bowing Court (M).** It generalises the user's own example,
   "lure the Olmec into a pillar", into a rule: every loud thing leans the
   columns, forever. You aim the fall, but so does he. It shares the noise
   signal with pick 5.

Runners-up: x5 The Hollow Child (S, the cheapest strong card), t1 The
Day-Count, p3 The Sweat-House, x7 The Swallowing Toad, p2 The Quicksilver
Court.

## Overlaps to merge

- **The Echo**: h3 and x3 are one idea. Build it once (pick 5).
- **The Jade Mask**: h4 is a possession hunter, a1 is a copying building.
  Pick one reading. a1's "copies you, only while lit" is the stranger of
  the two.
- **The Tithe-Taker**: h5 follows you, x1 follows the pile. Keep x1.
- **Mirrors**: h7, x6 and t6 all bend the Olmec's sight through a mirror.
  t6 is the cheapest frame for it, x6 the biggest payoff (a way to drop the
  Olmec off the layer).
- **Undoing breaks**: a3 and a7. Keep one.

## Shared tech the picks need

- **A wanderer-noise signal** the Olmec hears the way he hears a beacon:
  x1, x3/h3, x7, a2, a4, t3. Small, and the v1 brief already wants it.
- **A footprint-replay buffer**: h2, h5, h7.
- **Light queries on the torch** (is this point lit, by whom): h1, x5, p2,
  t2, a6.
