Main menu & world select, dealt as cards
Five menu cards in the 5a and 5d faces. They bob at rest; hover one and it lifts, tilts toward the cursor and its neighbours lean away. Click (or ↵) to draw it; arrow keys work too. Top row (7a, 7b) is world select: your six worlds plus a blank card along the foot, and the scene behind turns to whichever world you hover. Bottom row is the main menu; Your worlds deals into the same select, Esc or ◂ Menu goes back.
