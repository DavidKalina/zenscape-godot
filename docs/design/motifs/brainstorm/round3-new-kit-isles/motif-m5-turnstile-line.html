The Turnstile Line

motif-m5-turnstile-line.md · verb time a crossing; scale human; mood playful; shape a line (three isles in a row, each higher or lower than the last).

Three small isles in a row, west to east, and each hop to the next is a clock. A prayer-rope swing is paced by a pendulum, and a hung stair is walkable for only 12 s after you slam its boss. Do the line in one run and the pilot ends the lap with a stolen chest and a plane waiting.

Fit: role hero/line; footprint about 170 x 40 m (three isles end to end); height span about 25 m (nimbus 17 m above the Meadow, Court +8, Rest -6).

Play: You land on the Meadow's strip, walk to the prayer gate and take the ropes: a pendulum's own rhythm sets how fast you can go, a mistimed release overshoots and you twirl back. The lintel's top walks you onto the Court, where the hood's roof is a slide that funnels you straight at the boss: slam it and a 12 s clock starts that sends you down a dark stair to a vault and back. Nothing here hunts you; the line itself is the tension, and the feeling is a small, quick game of beat the bell.

Set pieces: - relic_torana (Meadow's east lip): five prayer ropes under a lintel that tilts up eight metres to the Court's rim; each release is timed against the swing, the last lands you on the lintel's top.
- relic_nimbus (Meadow's north edge): the broken ring and its climb up the inner left wall, spoke and outer brackets to the low horn, then a twirled leap to the high horn at 17 m; the shortcut is a sail from the high horn across the gulf to the Court, skipping the ropes. The plane can fly through the ring (11.4 m clear).
- pent_hood (Court's south wall): climb the wall, drop onto the roof's high edge and surf its 20 deg pitch down to the eaves, then slam the boss 3 m below. Its dark gallery under the eaves is a place to catch your breath.
- keel_hung_stair (Court, boss mid-turf, flight in a shaft): a slam on the boss turns the hung stair into a walkable flight for 12 s; at its foot a gallery 26 m long under the Court ends at a tomb_chest vault; run in, grab, run back before the flight swings home.

Moments

#momentwhat happens
1The first ropeYou swing off the prayer gate's lip and release rising to catch the next knot, the pendulum's own beat telling you when.
2The ring's hornA twirled leap to the nimbus's high horn: the whole line lies at your feet, and a sail across skips the ropes.
3Hood slideYou drop onto the hood's roof and surf its pitch down the eaves to the boss 3 m below.
4Slam, then 12 sA slam turns the hung stair; the bell of its grit tells you the clock is running.
5The vaultYou grab the tomb chest at the gallery's end and run the 26 m back, up the flight as it begins to swing.
6Surfed outMiss the clock and the hung flight tosses you out of the keel to sail down to the Rest.
Threat

only the stair's own throw (stood on it past 70 deg you are tossed off the Court into the gulf) and the gap. Miss the 12 s and the hung flight is a 50 deg slide that surfs you out of the keel into open air toward the Rest, so a miss costs the chest, not your life. A rope that snaps dry (the torana's `weathered` rope) sets a gap in the chain.

Kit asks

none (a perch the Court's slam can fall from is the hood itself; if relic_torana lands with fewer than five ropes, the card scales to 3).
