The Pilgrim's Ladder

motif-m1-pilgrims-ladder.md · verb climb (with a hide and a descent); scale human to colossal; mood sacred; shape a tall stack (four isles, each higher and further east than the last), the literal reading of the seed.

Four sky isles stepped up like a pilgrimage, one new piece for each station: the prayer lintel's ropes lift you off the landing isle, the broken nimbus carries you up to the horns, a hooded cloister hides you from the bird on the crown, and a slam from the horn turns the hung stair into the way down. The plane lands on the foot isle, flies through the ring on the way out, and the whole climb is one loop back to it.

Fit: role hero/vertical; footprint about 150 x 50 m; height span 22 m plus the ring's 17 m (horn 24 m).

Play: You land on the foot isle, walk to the jamb and take the ropes up under the lintel, each a little higher than the last, onto the lintel's top and across to the Halo. There the ring is a ladder of stubs, a spoke and brackets round to the horns, a long climb in the open. From the high horn you sail across to the Crown, where a bird circles; you creep under the pent hood along the north wall, and when you come in off the horn you slam the boss to turn the stair, then run down it before the twelve seconds are up. It leaves you a long way up and a long way from home, and the way back is down.

Set pieces: - relic_torana (Threshold's east lip): 4 ropes under a lintel that rises 1.6 m per rope to the Halo's lip; a dry rope snaps and throws you on, so the chain has a gap to twirl. The niche sills on the jamb are the slow way up.
- relic_nimbus (Halo's centre): the climb is inner stubs up the left wall, the spoke round the front, outer brackets, then the low horn and a twirled leap to the high horn. The plane flies through the 11.4 m ring (over the 7.0 toy opening).
- pent_hood (Crown's north wall, 2 bays): a dark gallery to crouch along under the bird's up-ray; one fallen bay is an open dash. Its roof is a pent slide off the eaves down to the Crown's lower step.
- keel_hung_stair (Crown's south lip, boss on the turf): hung, its soffit hangs off the lip as a 50 deg slide that dumps you into the sky; slammed (from the sail in off the high horn, 2.1 m above the boss), it turns into a stair down a shaft to the Cellar for twelve seconds. Standing on it when it turns back throws you.

Wrong Way Down

motif-m2-wrong-way-down.md · verb fall/descend + stay hidden (inverted); scale human; mood wrong; shape a hanging underside (a low isle tucked under a plate's lip).

A plate isle with a small isle hung under its south lip, and every new piece reads the wrong way first: the stair is a chute, the ring is a doorway onto a drop, the covered gallery is the trap and the one exposed place, its roof, is the safe one. The way home runs up a lintel's ropes and, for twelve seconds, down a stair that is hungry to throw you back out.

Fit: role accent/underside; footprint about 88 x 70 m; height span 24 m (Lid at 0, nimbus top 17, Under 7.5 below).

Play: You park on the north run and walk south to the ring, where the lip shows you the whole underside in one look. Down is by the nimbus (step through the ring and fall or sail 8 m onto Under's north strip) or by the hung stair (step on it from the shelf and it surfs you off into the hood). Under is small, shaded and tight; the hood looks like the one safe room and is the trap; the way home is the torana's ropes, a rise to the shelf, and the slam that turns the stair is also a short cellar you can raid and must leave quickly. It leaves the player a little uneasy and a little proud of reading it.

Set pieces: - relic_nimbus on the Lid's south lip, its hole facing the gulf. Passing through the ring from the north steps over the trough (0.8 m) and off the lip: the nimbus frames a fall. Its climb (inner rays, spoke, brackets, horns) is an optional stunt to the horns at 17 m, a view of the whole isle and a sail start.
- keel_hung_stair on the shelf: the boss at the shelf's south edge, the flight hung out on the drop at 50 deg (a chute, not a stair), landing him on Under's east end beside the hood. A slam from the Lid's lip (the perch, 2.5 m above the boss, over the 1.8 m of air a slam needs) turns it for 12 s: the flight goes into a shaft cut under the shelf, to a cellar (the only cover on the isle nothing can see into, one collectible). Stay past the hold and the tell (0.5 s) tips the flight back out: stood on it past 70 deg he is tossed (knock at a run) out over Under onto the hood's gallery.
- pent_hood on Under, two bays, one fallen gap. The gallery (floor to eaves 2.8-3.5 m, TOP_ROOM wide) is where a wind blaster's plate sits at its dead end; stepping on it makes the mouths nearest where he stands inhale and blast, and with the fallen bay open and the parapet at 1.59 the blast shoves him out the gap. The roof (a 20 degree crouch surf off the eaves) and the wall walk (4.93 m) are exposed in view of the Lid, and above the blast: they are the safe way east.
- relic_torana at Under's east end: the jamb on Under's lip, three ropes, the lintel tilted up toward the shelf, the far end a SET_OFF over the shelf's lip when the release is good, as the card describes. This is the way up. A poor release meets the lintel's face and its lip; a miss drops back to Under's grass (a short fall).

The Oilbird Stairs

motif-m4-oilbird-stairs.md · verb stay hidden, then fall/descend (and climb out); shape a tight cluster; scale intimate; mood oppressive.

Four small isles hang round one dark gulf, and the guacharo circles over every bare stretch of turf. You go down the cluster by the only roofs there are, a hood's gallery and a stair you must slam into place, and you climb out the long way, in the open, up a broken ring.

Fit: role hero/descent; footprint about 60 x 40 m; height span about 17 m (the Lid down to Footing), plus the guacharo's circle above.

Play: You land on the Lid's bare strip with the bird already circling, and the only way not to be taken is to get under stone: the hood on the Lid's south wall. From its wall walk you drop on the hung stair's boss, the stair swings into the notch under the lip, and you go down it hidden. Each isle below puts a roof over you and a sky-gap or an open lip between roofs, and you learn to move from cover to cover on the bird's schedule. Going back out is the opposite: no roof, a ring to climb and ropes over the gulf, with the guacharo reading you as fully exposed.

Set pieces: - `pent_hood` (Lid, 2 bays, fallen 0): the one clean hide, a gallery of about 10 m along the Lid's south wall. The bird loses you in it (cover ray hits the slabs), and the wall walk above it (4.9 m) is the perch for the slam.
- `keel_hung_stair` (Lid notch, then Gutter's east lip): hung it is a 50 degree slide off the lip, so you can't use it. Slam the boss from the hood's wall walk (4.9 m of air, over the 1.8 m needed) and it turns in about 1.7 s to a walkable flight under the turf, held 12 s, carrying you down into the notch of the isle below. The second one, on Gutter, is slammed from Gutter's own hood wall walk.
- `pent_hood` (Gutter, 2 bays, fallen 2): the rim walk with two bays missing, so two dashes through open sky under the bird's up-ray, with the hood's parapet as your only cover from the side.
- `relic_nimbus` (Footing west end): the exposed way out, inner stubs, the spoke round the front, outer brackets, then the twirl leap from the low horn to the high horn at +0.6, and a short jump from there to the Lid's rim. The plane fits through the ring.
- `relic_torana` (Gutter west to Understair's south rim): a lintel tilted up over the gulf with 3 prayer ropes, the shorter way up, but in plain sight of the bird for its whole length.

The Turnstile Line

motif-m5-turnstile-line.md · verb time a crossing; scale human; mood playful; shape a line (three isles in a row, each higher or lower than the last).

Three small isles in a row, west to east, and each hop to the next is a clock. A prayer-rope swing is paced by a pendulum, and a hung stair is walkable for only 12 s after you slam its boss. Do the line in one run and the pilot ends the lap with a stolen chest and a plane waiting.

Fit: role hero/line; footprint about 170 x 40 m (three isles end to end); height span about 25 m (nimbus 17 m above the Meadow, Court +8, Rest -6).

Play: You land on the Meadow's strip, walk to the prayer gate and take the ropes: a pendulum's own rhythm sets how fast you can go, a mistimed release overshoots and you twirl back. The lintel's top walks you onto the Court, where the hood's roof is a slide that funnels you straight at the boss: slam it and a 12 s clock starts that sends you down a dark stair to a vault and back. Nothing here hunts you; the line itself is the tension, and the feeling is a small, quick game of beat the bell.

Set pieces: - relic_torana (Meadow's east lip): five prayer ropes under a lintel that tilts up eight metres to the Court's rim; each release is timed against the swing, the last lands you on the lintel's top.
- relic_nimbus (Meadow's north edge): the broken ring and its climb up the inner left wall, spoke and outer brackets to the low horn, then a twirled leap to the high horn at 17 m; the shortcut is a sail from the high horn across the gulf to the Court, skipping the ropes. The plane can fly through the ring (11.4 m clear).
- pent_hood (Court's south wall): climb the wall, drop onto the roof's high edge and surf its 20 deg pitch down to the eaves, then slam the boss 3 m below. Its dark gallery under the eaves is a place to catch your breath.
- keel_hung_stair (Court, boss mid-turf, flight in a shaft): a slam on the boss turns the hung stair into a walkable flight for 12 s; at its foot a gallery 26 m long under the Court ends at a tomb_chest vault; run in, grab, run back before the flight swings home.

motif-m6-vesper-circuit.md · verb RIDE (the plane as much as the pilot), shape A RING, scale A FRAGMENT OF SOMETHING BIGGER, mood QUIET

Four isles are the surviving arcs of a colossal processional ring road, and the gates of the road still stand: a broken nimbus the plane flies through, a prayer lintel it flies under. The pilot on foot rides the same circuit in his own way (roof slide, rope swing, soffit surf, sail), and his climb crosses the plane's line twice, once inside the nimbus and once under the lintel.

Fit: role hero (a big quiet loop); footprint about 190 x 180 m (isles over a 130 m ring); height span about 30 m over the lawn plus 22 m of keel below isle 2.

Play: You start in the hood and ride your way round: slide the pent roof to the lawn or take the wall walk to the rope bridge, climb the broken nimbus in the plane's shadow, leap to the high horn and slam down onto the stair's boss, then run the turned stair into the undercroft before it folds. At the far isle you swing up the torana's ropes while the plane's loop passes under your boots, and sail home from the lintel's top. A second pilot's way is the plane's: thread the ring and the gate with nothing hanging on the stick. It is a circuit to be ridden twice, once on foot and once in the air, and both are quiet.

Set pieces: - relic_nimbus (isle 2): the plane flies through the ring (clear 11.4 m across, over WorldScale.opening(TOY_THREAD) 7.0) while the pilot climbs it: inner rays up the left wall, the spoke round the front, outer brackets, the low horn, then a twirled leap to the high horn (17.1 m over its foot, 1.6 m above the low horn).
- keel_hung_stair (isle 2): boss on the turf 6 m in front of the ring; hung, its soffit is a 50 degree slide off the lip. A flow-charge slam from the high horn (about 17 m of air) turns it (0.5 s tell, 1.2 s turn); it lies under the turf 12 s as a stair into the keel, then folds.
