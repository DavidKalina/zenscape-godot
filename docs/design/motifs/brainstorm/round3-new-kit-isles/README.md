## Kit-ask tally

1. **A shaft under the boss (4 cards: m2 asks outright; m3, m4, m5 invent
   one).** The turned hung stair lies under the turf, so it needs a lined
   cut, cellar or overhanging keel. KitLand.island has no hole. Whichever
   card is picked, the greybox must solve this first (a built plinth or a
   cliff-step edge works without a cut).
2. **A rope-bridge head on the hood's wall walk (m6).** A bridge_anchor on
   pent_hood's `top` connector.
3. **A perch above every boss (all cards).** A slam needs about 1.8 m of
   air: hood wall walks (m4, m5), the nimbus's high horn (m1, m3) or a lip
   (m2). This is a layout rule, not a new piece. m3's horn-to-boss slam is
   about 13 m away and unchecked.

## Spread pick: the three that differ most

- **m4 The Oilbird Stairs:** tight, hunted and downward, with the only real
  threat loop. Uses the hood as cover, as built.
- **m6 The Vesper Circuit:** wide, quiet, and the plane plays. The only card
  where the flown route matters.
- **m2 Wrong Way Down:** the contrarian found something. The hood as the
  trap and its roof as the safe way is a reading no other card has, and it's
  compact (88 x 70 m).

**The literal baseline (m1)** is beaten on character by m4 and m2. It's the
safest build, though: every piece is used exactly as built.

## Overlaps and weak spots

- m1 and m3 are near twins: the same name and the same stacked
  ropes-halo-stair ascent. m3 is the taller and tighter of the two. Pick
  one.
- m5's clock has no real penalty: a miss only costs the chest, and the
  8-riser stair makes the 12 s loose unless the gallery is about 26 m long.
- The torana's numbers are the card's, not the code's (its lane hadn't
  landed). Rope count and rise get re-checked in the plan.
- m2 and m4's falls (8 m, 11 m) haven't been checked against the motor's
  hard-fall rule.
