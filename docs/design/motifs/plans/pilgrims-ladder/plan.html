The Pilgrim's Ladder

plan.md ·

Four sky isles stepped up and east like a pilgrimage, one new piece at each station. The torana's ropes lift you off the landing isle. The broken nimbus is the climb up to its horns. A pent hood on the Crown hides you from the guacharo. A slam on the hung stair's boss turns its flight into the way down to the Cellar, and from there two easy hops and a sail take you home to the plane. The plane lands on Threshold, threads the torana gate and flies out through the ring.

Fit: role hero/vertical. Footprint about 160 x 45 m (x -50..107, z -14..24). Height span 0 (Threshold) to 20 (Crown), the high horn at 24.1. It stands alone in the sky. Keep 20 m clear round it for the plane, and 12 m clear of anything north and south of the nimbus so the plane can line up on the ring.

Play: You land on the foot isle, walk to the jamb and ride the ropes up under the lintel onto its top, then drop onto the Halo. You climb the nimbus's ring by its stubs, spoke and brackets to the low horn, and a twirled leap takes you to the high horn. From there you sail across to the Crown, where the bird wakes. You creep under the hood to the reliquary, then jump off the south lip and slam the boss, drop into the shaft and run down the turned stair before its 12 s run out. From the Cellar you sail down to the Halo and sail on home.

Set pieces: - relic_torana (the real piece), its jamb on Threshold's east lip at x 25: 4 SwingRopes, all 5.6 m, from iron arms on posts along the lintel's top front (+Z) edge, rings at x 28.2, 33.8, 39.4, 45.0 (heights 6.5, 8.1, 9.7, 11.3), z 1.58. Each knot is one SET_OFF higher than the last, and the 3rd rope is dry. The last rope's backswing release lands on the lintel's top (16 deg); the slow way is three offering steps up the jamb's head (6.4), a drop onto the lintel's root and a walk up its top to the snapped end (x 46.2, top 10.5), then a hop down onto the Halo (7).
- relic_nimbus, on the Halo at (62, 7, -3), 3 m north of its centre line so the torana's hop lands in front of the ring: the inner rays, the spoke, the outer rays, the low horn, then a twirled leap over the 3.9 m crown gap to the high horn. The plane threads the 11.4 m ring.
- pent_hood, along the Crown's north rim: 3 bays with the middle roof fallen. The gallery is cover from the bird's up-ray, the roof gap is an open dash, and the roof's 20 deg pitch is a surf down to the Crown's turf.
- keel_hung_stair, on a built plinth off the Crown's south lip. Hung, its soffit is a 50 deg slide off into the sky. Slammed from 4 m or more, it turns into a stair from the plinth's shaft down to the Cellar for 12 s, and it throws anyone still on it when it turns back.

6The cellarDown the shaft and the flight before it turns back and throws the late; then hop to the Halo and sail home.

Landing

Strip on Threshold's west half, x -38..-8 at z -5..5, a 30 m run due east on flat turf at 0, the approach from the west over the lip at x -50. A hand landing touches at 10.5 m/s or less and brakes at 4.2 + 0.9 m/s2, so it stops in about 11 m. The strip ideal is 18 m (PlaneStrip IDEAL). 30 m leaves about 19 m for the float and touch. The band x -45..-3, z -8..8 stays clear of pieces, flora and rocks (the torana jamb is at x 25, 33 m past the strip's end).
