# ZenScape: names for the ruins world

Brainstorm only. Starred items (★) are the favourites in each list. Tone: old, quiet, a little dangerous. Not cozy, not cute, not jokey. Avoided words: ancient, mystic, realm, forgotten.

## 1. Region one names

The gothic ruin-kit isles over the cloud sea.

1. ★ **Sundered Choir** — the halls stand in a broken ring, and the wind sings through the gaps.
2. **Cairnfall** — the isles dropped out of the sky in a line, and nobody counted who was on them.
3. **Hesk Tiers** — terraced halls stepping down into the cloud, each tier a little colder.
4. **Greyhollow** — the roofs are gone but the nave still holds the echo of bells.
5. **The Unroofed** — a region named for what the builders left open to the weather.
6. **Varrow Deep** — the sea of cloud under the isles, and the ruins that sink into it.
7. **Lanthorn Isles** — a few lamps still burn on the stone, and no one lit them.
8. **Stillwater Quay** — a harbour of isles where nothing moves but the drift of cloud.
9. **Seventh Arch** — the aqueducts count to seven, and the seventh is missing.
10. **Caul** — a thin skin of mist over everything, and under it the fallen halls.
11. **Ostrin Reach** — the long line of isles that runs from the sunrise to the storm.
12. **The Quiet Tiers** — the stone terraces where the wind never quite arrives.
13. **Halvard's Colonnade** — a single colonnade that runs for miles across the sky.
14. **Ember Wake** — the trail the isles leave in the cloud, glowing faintly at dusk.
15. **Tessary** — an old-sounding invented word for the sum of the isles' broken parts.

### 2a. Names for the people as a whole
