# Water mischief

Extend the existing God Mode playground with a complete playful interaction:
carry Bigfoot into the pond, dunk him with the cursor, release him to paddle to
a checked dry shore, shake off, react toward the player, and resume exploring.
He stays cartoon-resilient; there are no injury or drowning mechanics.

Keep the beautiful preset, tree, wind, camera, pause, reset, and cancellation
controls usable. Support grabbing him again during swimming or recovery.

Completion requires focused input and state-transition tests, shared-creature
and existing-playground regressions, visual inspection, and a demonstration
video checked for capture hitching. Record actual evidence and limitations.

Implemented and verified on `codex/god-mode-playground`. See `validation.json`,
`video-pacing.json`, and the local `water-mischief.mp4` demonstration.
